# Initial Concept
The primary user base for this street name mapping tool comprises community mappers and OpenStreetMap contributors. This tool aims to empower these users by streamlining the process of reconciling street names between OpenStreetMap and official LAMAS data, thereby improving data accuracy and consistency within the mapping community.

## Product Vision
To provide a robust and intelligent street name mapping pipeline that assists community mappers and OpenStreetMap contributors in accurately aligning street names with official LAMAS IDs. The tool leverages a multi-layered approach—including Unicode-based language detection, Arabic-to-Hebrew transliteration with AI refinement, advanced weighted fuzzy matching, distinctive word verification, synonym-aware de-duplication, topological analysis, and AI-driven settlement and street resolution—to handle complex Hebrew and Arabic naming variations and linguistic edge cases. The system prioritizes transparency through persistent diagnostic logging and explainable matching decisions surfaced in interactive reports, reducing manual effort and fostering a more precise, harmonized geospatial dataset for public use.

## Target Audience
The core users are community mappers and OpenStreetMap (OSM) contributors. These individuals are often volunteers passionate about improving geographical data, who may encounter challenges in standardizing street names due to variations in local spellings, abbreviations, and languages. The tool will serve as a valuable asset for them to enhance the quality of OSM data.
