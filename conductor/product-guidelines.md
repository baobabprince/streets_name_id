# Product Guidelines

## Tone and Style for User-Facing Documentation and Reports
The primary tone and style for all user-facing documentation and reports will be **friendly and encouraging**. This approach is specifically chosen to foster a welcoming and supportive environment for community mappers and OpenStreetMap contributors. We aim to:

*   **Be Accessible:** Use clear, straightforward language, avoiding overly technical jargon where possible, or providing clear explanations when technical terms are necessary.
*   **Be Supportive:** Frame instructions and feedback in a helpful and constructive manner, recognizing the voluntary nature of community contributions.
*   **Be Positive:** Emphasize the positive impact of their work and the collaborative spirit of the project.
*   **Encourage Engagement:** Design documentation to be inviting and to motivate users to engage with the tool and contribute to the community.

By adopting a friendly and encouraging tone, we intend to lower the barrier to entry for new contributors, maintain the enthusiasm of existing members, and ultimately enhance the overall quality and coverage of street name data within OpenStreetMap.
